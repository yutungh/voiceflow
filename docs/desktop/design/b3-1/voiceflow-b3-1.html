How the pill moves1280 × 800

How the pill moves

Motion only shows a change of state. It is quick (under 200 ms), uses no bounce, and turns off with Windows' reduce-motion setting.

Listening

1Appears

Press CtrlSpace. The pill rises 8 px and fades in.180 ms, ease-out

Listening

2Listens

Bars follow your voice live, so you can see the microphone works.Bars only, 60 fps

No sound

3No sound

Nothing coming in for 3 seconds: the bars go flat and the pill says so, with Choose microphone.Cross-fade, 200 ms

Tidying up

4Tidies up

Bars ease down to a resting line; a soft shimmer runs across them while the text is tidied.200 ms, then a 1.2 s loop

Pasted

5Pastes

The tick cross-fades in (scale 0.25 to 1, blur 4 to 0 px), holds 600 ms, then the pill slides down 8 px and fades.300 ms in, 150 ms out

Not pasted

6Needs you

A problem does not fade. The pill grows a line of text and one fix, and stays until you choose.Height change, 200 ms

Reduced motion: no slide and no shimmer. States swap instantly with the same icons and words, so nothing depends on movement.

Buttons: press shrinks them to 96% for 100 ms. Hover changes colour only. Nothing animates on ordinary clicks.
