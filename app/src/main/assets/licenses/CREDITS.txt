Changes made: the zi, jichu and duoyin dictionaries were merged; tone marks
were removed and ü written v; only the most frequent words were kept (plus
chat-shaped phrases); a short list of everyday chat words was given more
weight. The data is provided as is, with no warranties.
The Rime engine (0.9.0) uses the same three dictionaries unchanged, compiled
into Rime's binary format, plus a VoiceLnq table that lists those everyday
chat words again with their Wanxiang readings at 20 times their weight.
